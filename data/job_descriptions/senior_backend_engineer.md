# Senior Backend Engineer

## Description

We are seeking a Senior Backend Engineer to lead the design and implementation of scalable, distributed systems serving millions of users. You will work with a high-performing team to architect microservices, optimize database performance, and ensure system reliability at scale.

## Responsibilities

- **System Architecture**: Design and architect scalable backend systems supporting high concurrency and throughput
- **Code Leadership**: Review code, establish best practices, and mentor junior engineers
- **Performance Optimization**: Identify bottlenecks, optimize queries, and improve system performance
- **Cross-functional Collaboration**: Work with product, frontend, and infrastructure teams to deliver integrated solutions
