# Data Scientist

## Description

We are looking for a Data Scientist to develop machine learning models that drive business insights and product improvements. You will work with large datasets, build predictive models, and communicate findings to technical and non-technical stakeholders.

## Responsibilities

- **Model Development**: Build, train, and validate machine learning models for business problems
- **Data Analysis**: Perform exploratory data analysis to uncover patterns and insights
- **Experimentation**: Design and execute A/B tests and controlled experiments
- **Stakeholder Communication**: Present findings and recommendations to business stakeholders
